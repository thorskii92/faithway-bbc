For volunteers

Updating the church website

Everything on this page is done in the editor at /admin, from a phone or a computer. You don't need to know any code. After you save, the website updates by itself in about 2 minutes.

Step 1

Sign in

  1. Ask the website admin to add you as a helper. You'll get an email invitation from GitHub. Accept it (make a free GitHub account if you don't have one).
  2. Open the editor and click Sign In with GitHub.
  3. You'll see the list of things you can edit on the left: Events, Memory Verses, Videos, Announcements, Prayer Letters, Ministries and Church Info.
Every list shows Added with the date and time each entry was saved, so you can see what's new. It appears about a minute after saving; reload the page to see it.
Most common

Add an event

  1. Click Events, then New Event.
  2. Fill in the Title, Category, Date, Place and a one or two sentence Summary. Add the Time if you know it: it's used for "Add to calendar".
  3. Before the event: upload the Invitation poster. It appears under "You're invited" until the day passes, and becomes the picture people see when the event is shared on Facebook or Messenger.
  4. After the event: add Photos. The first photo is the cover; drag to reorder.
  5. Click Save.
Only know the month? Pick any day in that month and write it in Show date as, e.g. "November 2025".

Add a video

  1. On YouTube tap Share → Copy link. On Facebook open the video, tap ⋯ → Copy link (the video must be Public).
  2. Click Videos → New Video, paste the link, and fill in the title, type, date and who sang it.
  3. For Facebook videos, upload a Thumbnail (a screenshot of the video works). YouTube adds its own picture.
  4. Pick the Event (for example the cantata) to also show the video on that event's page.

Memory verse

  1. Memory Verses → New Memory Verse.
  2. Type the reference and the verse exactly as in the King James Bible.
  3. For Week of, pick the Sunday of that week. You can add a whole month in advance: each one shows when its week comes.

Announcements

Short notices for the "This week at Faithway" box on the home page: a schedule change, a fellowship lunch, a sign-up.

  1. Announcements → New Announcement.
  2. Write a short title and, if needed, one or two sentences.
  3. Set Show from and Show until. It appears on the first day and disappears by itself after the last day. Nothing to clean up.

Prayer letters

  1. Prayer Letters → New Prayer Letter.
  2. Pick the Missionary (listed by country), give it a title and the letter's date.
  3. Then do one of these: type or paste the letter, upload photos of the printed pages (in order), or attach the PDF.
Letters are public. Leave out anything that could put a missionary at risk: home addresses, exact locations, names of local believers, or anything the missionary marked private. Missionaries in sensitive countries are not in the list on purpose. Never post their letters anywhere on the website.

Ministries

Each card in the Ministries section can say when it meets, who it's for and who to ask. Open a ministry, fill in what you know and save. Use Order to arrange them (1 shows first).

Church info

Church Info → Phone and email holds the church phone number, email and the prayer request form link. They appear in the Visit section and the footer. Leave a field empty to hide it.

Please read

Photo rules

  • Only post photos taken at church events, and only ones the people in them would be happy to see online.
  • Children: group photos are fine. Don't post close-ups of a child together with their full name, school or home area. If a parent asks, don't post their child at all.
  • Never post photos of missionaries who serve in sensitive countries, even in a group photo.
  • If anyone asks for a photo to be removed, remove it the same day: open the event, delete the photo from Photos, and save. No questions asked.
  • Pick the best 10–30 photos per event rather than everything. Blurry or closed-eye shots can stay on the phone.

If something goes wrong

  • I saved but the website hasn't changed. Wait 2–3 minutes, then reload the page with Ctrl + F5 (on a phone, pull down to refresh).
  • I made a mistake. Open the entry again, fix it and save. To take something down quickly, switch on Hide from website (draft).
  • I deleted something by accident. Tell the website admin: every saved version is kept, so it can be brought back.
  • The editor says I can't sign in. Check that you accepted the GitHub invitation (look in your email).
