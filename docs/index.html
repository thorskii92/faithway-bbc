Our church covenant

Church Covenant

Having been led, as we believe, by the Spirit of God, to receive the Lord Jesus Christ as our Saviour, and on the profession of our faith, having been baptized in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, we do now in the presence of God, angels, and this assembly, most solemnly and joyfully enter into covenant with one another, as one body in Christ.

Read the full covenant ↓

We engage therefore, by the aid of the Holy Spirit, to walk together in Christian love; to strive for the advancement of this church, in knowledge, holiness, and comfort; to promote its prosperity and spirituality; to sustain its worship, ordinances, discipline, and doctrines; to contribute cheerfully and regularly to the support of the ministry, the expenses of the church, the relief of the poor, and the spread of the gospel through all nations.

We also engage to maintain family and secret devotion, to religiously educate our children; to seek the salvation of our kindred and acquaintances; to walk circumspectly in the world; to be just in our dealings, faithful in our engagements, and exemplary in our deportment; to avoid all tattling, backbiting, and excessive anger; to abstain from the sale and use of intoxicating drinks as a beverage, and to be zealous in our efforts to advance the kingdom of our Saviour.

We further engage to watch over one another in brotherly love; to remember each other in prayer; to aid each other in sickness and distress; to cultivate Christian sympathy in feeling and courtesy in speech; to be slow to take offence, but always ready for reconciliation, and mindful of the rules of our Saviour, to secure it without delay.

We, moreover, engage that when we remove from this place we will, as soon as possible, unite with some other church, where we can carry out the spirit of this covenant, and the principles of God’s Word.

What we believe

Standing on the Word of God

We are an Independent Bible Baptist Church. These are the truths we hold to, each grounded in Scripture (King James Version).

Draft for the pastor’s review

The Bible

The Holy Bible is the inspired, inerrant and preserved Word of God, our final authority in all matters of faith and practice. We use the King James Version.

2 Timothy 3:16 · Psalm 12:6–7

The Godhead

There is one true God, eternally existing in three persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost.

1 John 5:7 · Matthew 28:19

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is God the Son, born of a virgin. He lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead and ascended into heaven.

Isaiah 7:14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4

Salvation

Salvation is a free gift received by grace through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ alone, not by works. Those who are saved are kept by the power of God.

Ephesians 2:8–9 · John 10:28

The Ordinances

The Lord gave the church two ordinances: baptism by immersion of believers, and the Lord’s Supper in remembrance of His death.

Romans 6:4 · 1 Corinthians 11:23–26

The Local Church

The local church is an independent, self-governing body of baptized believers, called to worship God and to carry the Gospel to every nation.

Acts 2:41–42 · Matthew 28:19–20

The Lord’s Return

The Lord Jesus Christ will return personally and bodily for His own, and every believer should live ready for that day.

John 14:3 · 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17
